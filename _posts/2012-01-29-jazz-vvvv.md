Matteo Fraboni, a musician and composer asked me to create some interactive visuals to accompany his album "THIS IS MY MUSIC".

I used VVVV, a graphic programming language for Windows.

For every song I have designed an interactive environment which analyses the music, and transform it into an evolving structure.

Promo for the album and the real time graphics.
